Four directions for Klausul

Eight consumer money and insurance products scanned in the browser with a live style probe — computed type scales, tracking, radii and palettes, not adjectives. Then four directions built from what they actually do, each rendering the same finding so they compare like for like.

Why the last version read as an enterprise console. Your REFERENCE.md says a consumer app is the wrong reference for a B2B console. I applied it backwards — I took the clinical / evidence-led class and built an analyst tool for someone who opens this once a year. The scan shows exactly which decisions gave it away:

  • 14px body. Every product scanned sits at 16–19px. GOV.UK is 19px. N26 is 18px.
  • Hairline borders on every block. Alan, Monzo and N26 separate with space and fill. Borders on everything is the console tell.
  • −0.035em heading tracking. Six of eight use normal tracking at display sizes. Tight tracking is a B2B signature.
  • 10px radius. The field splits into soft (16–40px) or deliberate zero (0–4px). 8–12px is the enterprise middle I sat in.
  • Monospace micro-labels everywhere. None of the eight use mono outside of code.
  • Six things competing on one screen. Density follows frequency. Annual use means one idea per screen.
